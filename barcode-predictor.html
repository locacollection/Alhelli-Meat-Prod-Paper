Verify with a printed label

Scan the barcode on a real sticker, then read and enter its printed weight and total price independently. This checks whether the actual label details agree with the predicted code. A matching barcode alone does not prove the weight.

Verification is local to this page. No scan or printed values are automatically saved to Supabase or Production Paper. A match does not mean store checkout will accept the barcode.
